The hero's look (hood, long coat with the strip hem and filaments, visor, neon lines, wrist display, the gunblade, the
extra coat bones) is generated by our own Blender script `tools/hero/build.py` on top of the Quaternius Universal Base Characters male body.
The script was written with Claude Code (Anthropic), 2026-10-04.
The wardens' look (EW1 the sentinel: fitted armor shells, the hanging plates, the narrow helmet with its visor slit, the
energy halberd, the light lines; EW2 the enforcer: the broad armor, the hex energy shield, the short blade) is generated by
our own Blender script `tools/warden/build.py` (`--variant sentinel|enforcer`) on top of the Quaternius Universal Base
Characters male body, from the concept images EW1 and EW2. The drone (EM1: orb, shader iris, orbiting rings, scanning fan)
is built procedurally in `view/drones.ts`. Both were written with Claude Code (Anthropic), 2026-10-04.
Since 2026-10-05 the warden is drawn as a particle swarm (EW3g): the same skinned bodies, rendered by our own dot shader and a pooled
particle system (`view/swarm.ts`, written with Claude Code); the armor meshes of the build are dropped at load, the models are unchanged.

## Music (`audio/music/`)

All music files were generated with **Google Lyria 3** (Lyria 3 Pro for the loops, Lyria 3 Clip for `hack` and the stingers)
through OpenRouter, 2026-10-05, from our own text prompts (no artist names), by the team. Lyria returns a finished stereo mix;
we only pitch-shifted, trimmed, looped (cut on bar lines, loop-seam crossfade), loudness-matched (-16 LUFS, stingers -19) and
encoded the takes with ffmpeg. No other music or samples.

| --- | --- |

| `hack`, `sting_win`, `sting_death`, `sting_end` | Google Lyria 3 Clip via OpenRouter |

## Sound effects (`audio/sfx/`)

Two sources. Most files are procedurally synthesized by our own code in `tools/sfx/` (no AI audio models). The
footsteps, landing, warden steps, worm skitter, sword, rifle, hit / kill sounds, shield, pickups, spawn gate and alarms
are layered and processed (trim, EQ, pitch, fades, loudness match) with ffmpeg from the Kenney CC0 packs below
(`tools/sfx/library.ts`, file list in `tools/sfx/library-manifest.json`); many of them also carry a synthesized transient
or body layer made by the same code, and the sword swing, the player hit and the ammo drop are fully synthesized there (no
Kenney layer). `tools/sfx/audit.ts` measures every file. The tooling was written with Claude Code (Anthropic), 2026-10-04.

| Pack | Used for | Author, licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impact Sounds | footsteps, landing, warden steps, hits, worm deaths, the pickup glass tick (layers) | Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0 |
| Sci-Fi Sounds | rifle and drone shots, force fields, spawn gate, the drone-kill debris, slime (layers) | Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0 |
| Interface Sounds | the digital tick on the steps, glitch layers | Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0 |
| Digital Audio | energy sweeps, zaps, power-ups, alarms (layers) | Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0 |
