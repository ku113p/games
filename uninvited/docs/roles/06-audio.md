# Audio designer / composer guide - Uninvited

This role owns everything the player hears: sound effects, the mix, gameplay audio cues, the adaptive music and the beep voices.
Music and Sound are two of the seven jam scores (`AGENTS.md`), so this area weighs about as much as Mechanics and Story together.
Files: `audio/sfx/` (generated SFX and the sound board), `audio/music/` (music loops and stingers), `tools/sfx/` (our synthesizer),
`tools/music/build.ts` (placeholder loops), `scripts/music-manifest.ts` -> `view/music-files.ts`, `view/audio.ts` (the mixer),
`view/cues.ts` (live-synthesized cues), `view/music.ts` (the music player), `view/warden-sound.ts`, `view/hack/sound.ts`,
the `audio` block of `config.json`, and the sound lines of `CREDITS.md`. DESIGN.md section 13 is the brief.

## Principles

- **The sound is the noise rule.** In Mark of the Ninja players did not understand the AI until the noise ring equalled the real hearing radius. Footstep loudness and the noise radius the guards use must be one number. ([Game Informer - Mark of the Ninja afterwords](https://gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2012/10/15/afterwords-mark-of-the-ninja); [Destructoid interview](https://www.destructoid.com/interview-defining-2d-stealth-in-mark-of-the-ninja/))
- **Priority in the mix:** voice > player > enemies > music > ambience. Everything goes through group buses; the important thing ducks the rest (fast attack, soft release). ([Game Audio Theory: Ducking](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/audio/game-audio-theory-ducking); [bugnet: how to mix game audio](https://bugnet.io/blog/how-to-mix-game-audio-so-nothing-drowns-out))
- **Voice limits per category**, oldest voice dropped - eight footsteps at once read as mush. (same bugnet source)
- **A limiter on the master** (`DynamicsCompressorNode`) so a wave of 15 enemies never clips. ([MDN DynamicsCompressorNode](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/DynamicsCompressorNode))
- **Adaptive music = vertical layers + horizontal transitions:** synced loops of the same tempo and length; switch on a bar line with a crossfade, stingers cover seams; ~30 s loops are enough. ([bugnet: adaptive music](https://bugnet.io/blog/adaptive-music-a-beginners-guide); [Dynamic game music on the web](https://abratabia.com/web-game-audio/dynamic-game-music.php); [vertical layering](https://sounddesign.irpr.agency/glossary/vertical-layering/), [horizontal re-sequencing](https://sounddesign.irpr.agency/glossary/horizontal-resequencing/))
- **Ghostrunner's darksynth:** dark, heavy bass, a constant pulse, music as "the continuation of the katana strike" that pulls you into flow (secondary source, treat details with care). ([The Ongaku - Ghostrunner / Daniel Deluxe](https://www.theongaku.com/posts/ghostrunner-soundtrack-daniel-deluxe))
- **Variation:** a random variant (never the same twice in a row) plus a small random pitch (about +-5%) on frequent sounds. General game-audio practice, no single source.

## Decisions for Uninvited

- **Style** (DESIGN 13, 15): music in the spirit of Ghostrunner (darksynth), generated by us with Lyria via OpenRouter. Tracks: two gameplay tracks per level, a hacking track (DESIGN 11: "its own music while hacking"), an office track, a calm room track. The second track per level is on PLAN section 3's cut list.
- **Voices** (DESIGN 13): beep voices with subtitles (Final Fantasy Tactics style); `voice_may/jim/johnny/steve` (6 variants each) exist. May's target is GLaDOS (DESIGN 10); real voices only if time allows.
- **No Halloween dressing** (DESIGN 14): no organs, theremins, ghost choirs or spooky clichés. "Uninvited" is carried by the story: rain, silence and a knock in the room; a cold, indifferent network.
- **Glitches when you break something must feel great**, and every action has a sound (DESIGN 13).
- **The endings are quiet** (DESIGN 4, changed 2026-10-04): no triumph sting. Peaceful: the letter, later a small parcel. Sad: a knock, "a parcel for you", black screen, the empty desk. Treat silence as part of the score.
- **Cones and noise rings are visible only in network vision** (DESIGN 8), so outside it sound is the main warning: direction (pan), the suspicion tone, the "spotted" sting.
- **~20-minute game** (DESIGN 6): ~5 min per level, ~5 min of real-world scenes. A 60 s loop repeats about 5 times per level; prefer longer loops.
- **The mixer as built** (`view/audio.ts`, `config.json audio.mixer`): buses player 1.0 / enemy 0.85 / ui 1.0 / music 0.55 / ambience 0.8 -> master (0.75) -> low-HP lowpass -> limiter (threshold -10 dB, knee 6, ratio 8, attack 3 ms, release 0.15 s). Voice limits: kill 4, footstep 3, hit 5, shot 4, glitch 3, derez 3, default 10, total 36; derez at most once per 80 ms, glitch per 50 ms; pitch jitter 5%; StereoPanner width 0.85; the drone hum and camera servo use the 3 nearest sources as panned loop voices; the world ducks -9 dB under a hack.
- **Cues as built** (`view/cues.ts`, `audio.cues`): a continuous suspicion tone 180-560 Hz following the strongest watcher, a "spotted" and an "all clear" sting (3 s apart at least), a 2 s wave-incoming riser and a wave-cleared sting; low HP (below 30%) gives a heartbeat 62-112 BPM, a 900 Hz muffle and -4 dB on the music. Footstep loudness follows `noiseRadius / noise.run` (8 m) between floor 0.18 and 1.0 (crouch from 0.14).
- **The music player as built** (`view/music.ts`, `audio.music`): one shared clock, 120 BPM, 4/4; every change happens on a bar line as a 1.2 s crossfade; intensity drops only after holding 6 s; -6 dB duck under a hack, May and the pause menu. Intensity: 0 calm (hidden), 1 tension (suspicion over 0.15, not hidden, alarm 1-2), 2 combat (alarm 3 or a wave). It accepts either four vertical stems `net_a.stem1-4` or three horizontal versions `net_calm/net_tension/net_combat`, plus `hack`, `room`, `office`, `menu`, `sting_win`, `sting_death`, `sting_end`. Missing files fall back (hack -> level track at tension; stingers -> a synthesized stand-in). Today `audio/music/` holds 16 s placeholder loops from `tools/music/build.ts`.
- **Settings:** master / music / SFX sliders exist (`view/settings-ui.ts`, defaults 1.0 / 0.7 / 1.0).

Where the review disagrees with DESIGN.md, DESIGN.md wins: the review's "victory" stinger becomes a quiet ending sting.

## Sources of sound

**Music - Lyria 3 on OpenRouter.** Two models: **Lyria 3 Pro** (~$0.08 per full song) and **Lyria 3 Clip** (~$0.04 per 30 s clip). Use Pro for the long loops (level, room, office, menu) and Clip for the hack loop, stingers and cheap drafts of a prompt. Lyria returns a finished stereo mix, not stems, so we use **horizontal versions** (`net_calm` / `net_tension` / `net_combat`), not `net_a.stem1-4`.
Budget: $3.71 of $10 spent (PLAN 5), **the key expires 2026-10-11**, so every track must be generated by 10-10. Rough cost: ~10 songs x 3 takes on Pro (~$2.40) plus ~6 stingers x 2 on Clip (~$0.50), about $3.
Credit Lyria (Google, via OpenRouter) in `CREDITS.md` under a new Music section the day the first track lands.

**SFX - no generator on OpenRouter**, so sound effects come from two places:
1. **Our synthesizer** `tools/sfx/` (130 files, deterministic, rebuilt with `bun tools/sfx/build.ts <word>`). The designer's verdict after playing: **only part of the library is good**. The **hack sounds are fine** (`hack_select/correct/wrong/success/fail/start/tick_loop`); **the footsteps are bad**.
2. **CC0 libraries** for whatever the synth does badly, physical sounds first: [Kenney Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) (footsteps on concrete/tile, impacts), [Kenney Sci-Fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds) (lasers, force fields, engines), [Kenney Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds), [Kenney Digital Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio); [Freesound](https://freesound.org/) filtered to CC0 only. CC0 needs no attribution, but the jam does: list every file's pack in `CREDITS.md`.

**How to replace a sound:**
- Triage on the sound board (`python3 -m http.server -d audio/sfx 8000`): the designer marks each group keep / fix / replace. Known so far - keep: hack_*; replace: footstep_run, footstep_sneak; unrated: the rest. Rate first what plays most often: footsteps, sword swing/hit, rifle shot, land, jump, dash, bullet impact, player hit.
- Footsteps: take 4-5 Kenney concrete/tile steps per group, high-pass them (~150 Hz), add a very quiet synth tick on top so they still sound like the network, loudness-match the set.
- Convert to the pipeline format: mono, 44.1 kHz, MP3 `libmp3lame -q:a 4`, silence trimmed, the same name pattern (`footstep_run_v1.mp3`) so `view/audio.ts` needs no change.
- `tools/sfx/build.ts` writes `audio/sfx/<name>.mp3` and the README. Remove a sound's recipe from `tools/sfx/sounds.ts` before you put a library file under its name, or the next rebuild overwrites it. Then fix the README text and the `CREDITS.md` line that say "no samples, sound libraries".

## Music plan

All tracks 120 BPM, D minor, so crossfades never clash (`audio.music.bpm` = 120).

| Track (file) | Length | Content | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `net_calm` | 32 bars ~64 s or longer, loop | pad + sub-bass pulse, no drums | Stealth (intensity 0) |
| `net_tension` | same bar count | + drums, sparse arp | Suspicion, alarm 1-2 (intensity 1) |
| `net_combat` | same bar count | full, aggressive mix, hits | Alarm 3, waves (intensity 2); switch on a bar line |
| `hack` | 16 bars ~32 s | ticking rhythm without bass, rising arp | The hack overlay; a faster layer at `lowTimeSec` if time allows |
| `room` | 60-90 s loop | pad, quiet music box / piano, no drums | The junkyard room |
| `office` | 60 s | cold pad, pulse | The prologue |
| `menu` | 60 s | may reuse `net_calm` | Title / pause |
| `sting_win` / `sting_death` / `sting_end` | 3-6 s | quiet end of level, death, sad final chord | Level end, death, endings |

If Lyria's three versions do not match in tempo or key, the fallback is one take, cut into sections by bars with ffmpeg and crossfaded on bar lines (horizontal only) - never layer unsynced files. Normalize all music files to one loudness so crossfades do not jump; the music bus (0.55) keeps it under the SFX, with -6 dB under May and the hack.

## Rules of thumb and metrics

- Every balance number lives in `config.json audio`; never tune one sound "by ear" in code.
- A new sound gets a bus and a voice-limit group in `view/audio.ts` (the `P(bus, group, jitter)` table). Frequent sounds get jitter; UI and stingers do not.
- A positional sound passes its world position so it is panned; anything a guard can hear also exists as a noise event in `core/` - the sound never invents a noise the rules do not have.
- One-shots peak at about -1 dBFS, dense sounds keep their loudest 50 ms under -9 dBFS RMS, loops -3 to -16 dBFS (`audio/sfx/README.md`).
- Loops play through Web Audio `loop = true`, never `<audio loop>`; check the seam on the board.
- Music: one tempo, one key, bar-aligned lengths (one bar = 2 s at 120 BPM). Check each take against a 120 BPM click; up to ~3% off can be fixed with ffmpeg `atempo`, more means a reroll.
- After `audio/music/` changes, run `bun run music:manifest` (also part of `bun run check`).
- The wave test: a 15-enemy alarm-3 wave must never clip, and `player_hit`, the worm windup and the "spotted" sting must stay audible.
- Silence is a tool: the room, the ending and the moment after a death are quieter than any gameplay.

## Don'ts

- No middleware and no HRTF `PannerNode` on 20 sources - StereoPanner is enough.
- Do not generate music in different tempos or keys "to fix later" - the crossfades will not fit.
- Do not layer separately generated takes as if they were stems.
- Do not tune single sounds by ear in code instead of buses, limits and `config.json`.
- Do not drop a library file over a synthesized name without removing its recipe - the next `tools/sfx/build.ts` run overwrites it.
- Do not spend days on new synth recipes for physical sounds (steps, impacts) - take CC0 ones.
- Do not use non-CC0 or unclear-license packs, and do not ship any asset that is not in `CREDITS.md`.
- No Halloween clichés, no triumphant ending music.
- Do not leave music generation for the last days: the OpenRouter key expires 2026-10-11.
